词条 · Goth 与 Gothic

Glossary: Goth and Gothic

两个词形，四层含义，按文献年代并列如下，不构成叙事。

Two word-forms, four recorded senses, listed in order of appearance.

3–8 世纪
Gothi：东日耳曼部落名，见于罗马史料。A tribal name of East Germanic peoples, recorded in Roman sources.
1140s 起
Gothic：建筑术语；文艺复兴时期意大利作者用以指称中世纪北方建筑，文献中初为贬义。An architectural label applied by Renaissance Italian writers, initially pejorative.
1764
贺拉斯·沃波尔《奥特兰托城堡》副题 “A Gothic Story”，文学类型义确立。Walpole’s subtitle fixes the literary sense.
1970s 末
gothic rock 及其后继用法；Goth 转而指称亚文化成员。gothic rock and the later subculture sense.

据《牛津英语词典》词源条；艺术史通论文献。
Sources: Oxford English Dictionary, etymological entries; standard surveys of art history.
