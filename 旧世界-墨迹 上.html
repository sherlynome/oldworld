Cat.12

敦煌遗书

The Dunhuang Manuscripts
材质纸质写本与印本（卷子本为主）
材质Paper manuscripts and woodblock prints, mostly in scroll (juanzi) format
描述1900 年 6 月 22 日，王圆箓发现莫高窟第 17 窟藏经洞。遗书总数约六七万件，各机构编目口径不同。年代上起魏晋，下至宋初，佛经约九成。唐咸通九年（868）《金刚经》为世界最早有明确纪年雕版印刷品。1907 年斯坦因、1908 年伯希和先后自藏经洞携取部分遗书。现分藏：中国国家图书馆 16000 余件、英国国家图书馆 17000 余号、法国国家图书馆 7000 余号、俄罗斯藏 19870 号等。20 世纪中国古文献四大发现之一。
描述On 22 June 1900 Wang Yuanlu discovered Library Cave 17 at Mogao. The corpus numbers roughly sixty to seventy thousand items, though cataloguing standards differ among institutions. Spanning the Wei–Jin period to the early Song, some ninety percent are Buddhist texts. The Diamond Sutra of Xiantong 9 (868) is the world's earliest securely dated woodblock print. Stein (1907) and Pelliot (1908) each removed portions of the collection; items are now divided among the National Library of China (16,000+), the British Library (17,000+), the Bibliothèque nationale de France (7,000+), and Russian collections (19,870). One of the four great discoveries of ancient Chinese documents in the twentieth century.
来源：各馆敦煌文献目录；敦煌研究院　|　Source: catalogues of the holding institutions; Dunhuang Academy

Cat.13

双钩廓填 · 万岁通天帖

Double-Tracing: The Wanshitongtian Copies
材质纸本摹帖
材质Ink tracing copies on paper
描述唐万岁通天二年（697），王方庆献王氏一门二十八人墨迹十卷，武则天命弘文馆双钩摹制。唐代原名《宝章集》，"万岁通天帖"之名系南宋岳珂著录所定。今存七人十帖，含王羲之《姨母帖》《初月帖》。历明真赏斋、清乾清宫两次火劫，火痕犹存。米芾评"下真迹一等"。1922 年溥仪携往长春，1954 年入藏辽宁省博物馆。（摹写之别，参上卷 Cat.10）
描述In 697 (Wanshitongtian 2) Wang Fangqing presented ten scrolls of traced originals by twenty-eight members of the Wang clan; Empress Wu ordered the Hongwen Institute to produce double-traced copies. Originally titled Baozhang Ji, the name "Wanshitongtian" was fixed by Yue Ke in the Southern Song. Seven hands and ten letters survive, including Wang Xizhi's Geng Postscript and First Month. Twice scarred by fire — at Zhenshang Studio (Ming) and Qianqing Palace (Qing) — the burn marks remain. Mi Fu judged them "one grade below the originals." Taken to Changchun by Puyi in 1922, they entered the Liaoning Provincial Museum in 1954.
来源：辽宁省博物馆　|　Source: Liaoning Provincial Museum

Cat.14

碑与拓

Stele and Rubbing
材质石刻与拓本
材质Stone inscription and ink rubbing
描述传拓一般认为隋代已产生，《隋书·经籍志》载"其相承传拓之本，犹在秘府"。现存最早有明确纪年拓本为唐太宗《温泉铭》（贞观二十二年／648 刻石，1900 年敦煌藏经洞发现，现藏法国国家图书馆）。全形拓为清嘉道间马起凤所创。
描述Rubbing is generally held to have arisen by the Sui dynasty: the Sui shu, Jingji zhi records that "transmitted rubbings were still kept in the imperial archives." The earliest dated extant rubbing is Taizong's Wenming of the Hot Springs (stone cut 648; found in 1900 in Library Cave 17; now in the BnF). Quanxing ta (full-form rubbing) was created by Ma Qifeng in the Jiaqing–Daoguang era.
来源：《隋书·经籍志》；故宫"宋拓魅力"展；上博"与时偕行"展

Cat.15

淳化阁帖 · 刻帖

The Chunhua Pavilion Model-Calligraphy
材质刻帖拓本
材质Engraved model-calligraphy and rubbings
描述淳化三年（992），宋太宗命王著编次摹勒，为中国最早的丛帖，后世称"法帖之祖"。十卷收 103 人 420 件，六至八卷为王羲之。原版于 1032 年皇宫火灾中焚毁。2003 年上海博物馆以 450 万美元自安思远处购回第四、六、七、八卷最善本。明肃府本刻石今存兰州。编者王著"识鉴不精，真伪杂糅"，须如实记之。
描述In 992 Emperor Taizong of Song ordered Wang Zhu to compile and cut the earliest collective model-calligraphy anthology, later honored as "ancestor of fatie." Its ten scrolls hold 420 pieces by 103 hands, scrolls 6–8 devoted to Wang Xizhi. The original blocks burned in the palace fire of 1032. In 2003 the Shanghai Museum bought back the finest surviving scrolls 4, 6, 7 and 8 for US$4.5 million from Robert Ellsworth; the Ming Suzhou (Su-fu) recut stones survive in Lanzhou. Editor Wang Zhu's "discernment was unrefined; genuine and forged pieces mingled" — recorded here as fact.
来源：上海博物馆　|　Source: Shanghai Museum

Cat.16

印章与篆刻

Seals and Seal-Carving
材质铜玺、石印、印泥
材质Bronze and stone seals, cinnabar paste
描述殷墟出土商代铜玺 3 件，于省吾《双剑簃古器物图录》著录；学界对"商玺"尚有争议，一说商代，至少春秋战国已普遍使用。秦传国玉玺"受命于天既寿永昌"传李斯篆，实物早失，历代传讹，此处仅作文献叙述。汉以后钤朱于纸。元王冕以花乳石刻印，为印材革新。2009 年中国篆刻入选联合国人类非物质文化遗产代表作名录。
描述Three Shang bronze seals from Yinxu are recorded in Yu Shengwu's Shuangjian yigu qiwu tulu; their dating remains debated — "perhaps Shang," at least in general use by the Spring-and-Autumn / Warring States periods. The Qin imperial jade seal reading "受命于天既寿永昌," attributed to Li Si's seal script, is lost; all later examples are spurious — treated here as textual tradition only. From Han onward seals were stamped in cinnabar on paper. Wang Mian's use of huaru stone (Yuan) reformed seal materials. In 2009 Chinese seal-carving was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
来源：《周礼·地官》；《双剑簃古器物图录》；篆刻史通论

Cat.17 · 尾声 · 对照站

王祯转轮排字盘

Wang Zhen's Revolving Typecase
材质木活字与转轮
材质Wooden movable type and revolving typecase
描述王祯于 1295 年任旌德县尹，1298 年（大德二年）制木活字三万余枚并转轮排字盘，印《旌德县志》"不出一月，百部齐成"——该县志今已失传。《造活字印书法》附于《农书》（1313 年刊），为世界最早系统叙述活字印刷的文献。"以人寻字则难，以字就人则易"为王祯原文。对照线（只列年代，不评优劣）：毕昇泥活字（北宋）→ 王祯木活字（1298）→ 古腾堡铅活字（约 1440 年代）。参 Vol.05 Cat.10。
描述Wang Zhen, magistrate of Jingde from 1295, in 1298 (Dade 2) cut over 30,000 wooden types with a revolving typecase and printed the Jingde County Gazetteer — "a hundred sets complete within a month" — that gazetteer itself now lost. His Method of Making Movable Type for Printing, appended to the Nong shu (printed 1313), is the world's earliest systematic account of movable-type printing. "To seek characters by walking round is hard; to bring the characters to the man is easy" is Wang Zhen's own words. Comparative line (dates only, no ranking): Bi Sheng's clay type (N. Song) → Wang Zhen's wooden type (1298) → Gutenberg's metal type (c. 1440s). Cf. Vol.05 Cat.10.
来源：王祯《农书·造活字印书法》；泾县／旌德官方史料
